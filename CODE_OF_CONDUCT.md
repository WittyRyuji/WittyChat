# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Be respectful, constructive and specific; critique code and ideas, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, deliberate
disruption and publication of another person's private information are not allowed.
Do not post customer data or secrets, even when reporting a bug.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, pause a discussion or restrict
participation, proportionate to impact and repeated behavior. Maintainer conduct
is subject to the same expectations.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner's available contact channel on
[GitHub](https://github.com/WittyRyuji); agree a private channel before sending
sensitive evidence. Report security issues through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not retaliate against good-faith reports.

欢迎不同背景的参与者。讨论聚焦事实、代码和设计，禁止人身攻击、歧视、骚扰及泄露隐私。
维护者可按影响采取提醒、移除内容或限制参与等措施，也应遵守同样的准则。
